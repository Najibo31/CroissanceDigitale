# **App Name**: Digitalis

## Core Features:

- Navigation Menu: A responsive navigation menu with links, a logo placeholder with subtle animation on hover, and a futuristic design with neon glow on hover for links.
- Footer: Footer with links, social icons with hover animations, and a copyright notice, designed with a sticky or wave animation for a modern touch.
- Homepage Hero Section: A hero section with animated text reveal, particle background, parallax scrolling, and buttons linking to services and contact form.
- Services Section: Service cards with hover effects and futuristic icons representing digital solutions, arranged in a grid.
- Team Section: Animated cards sliding in on scroll, holographic profile images with glow and distortion effects, showcasing team members and their expertise.
- Approach Section: A timeline or stepper displaying the company's approach with animated progress lines.
- AI Powered Review Display: Generative AI will select customer quotes for use on landing pages and success stories; it can also access a tool to contact them for permission.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Vibrant green (#009c6d) for calls-to-action and key elements, representing growth and innovation.
- Background color: Light gray (#e8e8e8) providing a neutral backdrop to make the vibrant green elements stand out.
- Accent color: Golden yellow (#bbad67) for highlights and accents, adding a touch of luxury and sophistication.
- Body font: 'Inter', a grotesque-style sans-serif for a modern, machined look, ideal for body text.
- Headline font: 'Space Grotesk', a sans-serif with a techy, scientific feel suitable for headlines. Pair with Inter for body text.
- Use futuristic, abstract digital motifs in #009c6d for service cards.
- Incorporate parallax scrolling, smooth animations with Framer Motion or GSAP, and interactive elements with hover effects like glows or neon accents.
